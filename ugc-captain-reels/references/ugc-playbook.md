# High-performing UGC playbook

What tends to work in short-form UGC on TikTok and Reels, distilled from current industry guidance (see Sources). The creator profile decides *how it looks and sounds*. This playbook decides *which format fits the footage and the brief*.

A note on evidence: the patterns below are consistent across many sources. The exact percentages floating around (hook rates, "X% decided in 3 seconds") mostly come from vendor blogs and conflict with each other, so treat any numbers as rules of thumb and never quote them in client deliverables.

## Principles that hold across sources

1. **The hook decides everything.** Viewers decide in roughly the first 1–3 seconds. Spend most of the effort there; a weak opening can't be rescued later.
2. **Open on tension, not setup.** Lead with the result, a contestable claim, or an open question. No greetings, intros or logos.
3. **Product early.** For shop and affiliate content, the product should be visible in the first frame or by about 3 seconds. TikTok Shop guidance reportedly also wants the hook to relate to the featured product.
4. **Show, don't list.** Demonstrate the product solving one specific pain; don't recite specs. Put key benefits in short text overlays.
5. **Native beats polished.** Handheld, face-to-camera, real setting. A studio look signals "ad" and gets scrolled.
6. **Keep changing the picture.** A new angle, cut, B-roll or punch-in every 2–3 seconds.
7. **Captions on every beat.** Many people watch muted, especially on Meta. Sound-on moments like ASMR or reactions are a TikTok bonus, not a replacement.
8. **Earn sends and rewatches.** On Reels, watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach are the main signals, and replays count. A last line that loops back to the hook, or a "send this to someone who…" moment, helps.
9. **Original only.** Both platforms suppress reposted or recycled content. Never reuse another creator's footage or lines.
10. **Test hooks, not whole videos.** Film one body and 3+ openers, then render each as its own variant. For paid ads, the 3-second view rate ("hook rate") is the metric to compare.

## Length

- **Paid ads and Spark Ads:** 21–35s is the most commonly cited sweet spot for conversion. Default to ~30s.
- **Organic educational, story or podcast-style:** 35–60s is fine *if* every beat earns its place.
- When over target, cut a middle beat. Never cut the hook, the product reveal or the CTA.

## Format library

Pick the format that matches the footage and the brief. Timings are for a ~30s cut; scale the middle beats for longer videos.

### 1. Testimonial / "I was wrong" (default for most product ads)
| time | beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2s | Result or confession hook, with the product in hand or in frame |
| 2–7s | Life before: the specific pain |
| 7–20s | What it is and how it fixed it, B-roll on every claim |
| 20–26s | Proof: their own result, a review screenshot, before/after |
| 26–30s | CTA |
Footage needed: talking head, product close-ups, usage shots.

### 2. Problem → Agitate → Solve (PAS)
0–3s pain callout ("If [problem] keeps ruining [moment]…") → 3–8s agitate (why the usual fixes fail) → 8–24s solution demo → 24–30s CTA. Best for problem-aware buyers and practical products.

### 3. "3 reasons why" / listicle
0–2s hook naming the payoff ("3 reasons I'll never go back to…") → three fast beats of 6–8s each, each with its own B-roll and a numbered text label → CTA. Easy to film, easy to pace, and the numbers keep viewers waiting for #3. Put the strongest reason first or last, never in the middle.

### 4. Unboxing / first impressions
0–2s sealed package and an anticipation line → 2–10s opening, with tactile close-ups (sound-on helps on TikTok) → 10–24s first use and honest reaction → 24–30s verdict and CTA. Curiosity opens it and proof closes it. Strong for gifts, premium packaging and subscription boxes.

### 5. Demo / before-after
0–2s the "after" first (pattern interrupt) → 2–6s rewind to "before" → 6–24s the process in quick B-roll steps → 24–30s side-by-side and CTA. Works with minimal talking; lean on text overlays.

### 6. Podcast-style clip
Looks like a moment from a show: seated, visible mic, conversational, calmer and more credible tone. Hot-take or "we were just talking about…" hook → story → product as a natural recommendation → soft CTA. Good for considered purchases, apps, services and higher-trust categories. See `creators/nate-flake.md` for the look.

### 7. Reaction / green-screen callout
Creator reacts to a review, comment, competitor claim or myth. 0–2s show the thing being reacted to (screenshot) with a hot take → 2–20s breakdown → product as the answer → CTA. In this pipeline, the screenshot is an image `cover` over the talking audio, or a short image segment; see `edit-plan.md`. Strong on TikTok, weaker on Meta, where it reads as platform-native.

### 8. Objection handler (retargeting)
0–2s "I thought [objection] too, until I saw [mechanism]" → 2–20s address the objection with proof → CTA. Use for brands that say viewers know the product but don't buy.

## Hook bank (patterns; write them in the UGC Captain's voice)

| type | pattern |
|---|---|
| Result first | "This [product] [specific result] in [timeframe]." |
| Confession | "I was so wrong about [category]." |
| Contrarian | "Unpopular opinion: [common belief] is wrong." |
| Pain callout | "If you [specific pain], stop scrolling." |
| Mistake | "Most people trying to [goal] make this one mistake." |
| Objection | "I thought [objection] too, until [mechanism]." |
| Curiosity gap | "I didn't expect [product] to [surprising thing]…" |
| Listicle | "3 reasons I'll never go back to [old way]." |
| Comparison | "[Expensive thing] vs [this]. I tested both." |
| POV/identity | "As a [identity], this is the only [thing] that…" |
| Overheard/podcast | "We were just talking about this off-mic…" |

Rules: be specific (numbers, timeframes, named pain), keep it to one breath (≤ 2s), match the text overlay to the spoken hook, and make sure the hook is about *this* product.

## CTA patterns
- Shop/affiliate: "Tap the orange cart / link below, it's on sale right now."
- Link in bio: "Link's in my bio if you want to try it."
- Soft/organic: "Send this to the friend who [pain]." This also drives sends on Reels.
- Always disclose paid partnerships with the platform toggle and/or #ad.
